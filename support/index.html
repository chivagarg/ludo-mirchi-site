Frequently Asked Questions

Does Ludo Mirchi require internet?

No. The game is fully offline on iPad.

Does Ludo Mirchi have ads?

No banner ads, video ads, or forced ads.

Can I play alone?

Yes. Set any player slot to a robot opponent.

How do pawn unlocks work?

Earn coins by playing, or unlock pawns faster with optional in-app purchases. Purchases are never required to play.

How do I restore purchases?

Use Restore Purchases in the app if shown, or email us for help.
